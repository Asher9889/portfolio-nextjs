# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and potential clients visiting to evaluate Saurabh for hire or collaboration. They've seen dozens of generic dev portfolios — this one needs to stop the scroll. They're multi-tasking on a laptop, often at the start or end of a workday, scanning quickly. If the site doesn't impress in 3 seconds, they move on.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that functions as a living credential. The design itself demonstrates the craft Saurabh brings to client projects — every interaction, transition, and layout choice is proof of ability. Success means visitors remember the site (not just the work) and feel compelled to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Confident, Playful. The voice is assured without arrogance — "Creative Engineer Building The Future" sets the tone. Playful details (typewriter text, orbiting icons, micro-interactions) keep it human. Not stoic, not corporate, not overly poetic.

## Anti-references

No generic dev portfolios. No hero + skills bars + contact form template. No gradient text, no glassmorphism-as-default, no dark theme cliché. If it looks like it came from a Bootstrap theme or a "10 modern portfolio templates" article, scrap it.

## Design Principles

1. **Craft as credential** — Every pixel proves skill. The interface itself is the strongest portfolio piece.
2. **Bold without noise** — Confidence through clarity and restraint. Playful but purposeful.
3. **One memorable moment per section** — Each scroll stop earns its place with a distinct visual or interaction.
4. **Never generic** — If it looks like a template, rewrite it. No assumptions about what a portfolio "should" be.
5. **Performance is polish** — Buttery scroll, instant interactions. No jank, no layout shift. Awe means nothing if it stutters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG level required. Avoid obvious issues: sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation on interactive elements, respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for non-essential animations. Maintain readability at all viewport sizes.
