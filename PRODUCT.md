# Product

register: brand

## Product purpose
A personal portfolio website that showcases the owner's work, process, and contact path in a clean, memorable way.

## Users
- Potential clients
- Recruiters
- Collaborators

## Tone
- Confident
- Crafted
- Technical
- Concise

## Principles
- Show one strong featured project first.
- Support it with smaller proof points, not a generic card grid.
- Keep copy short and specific.
- Make every section feel deliberate and scannable.
- Use motion and hierarchy to guide attention, not decoration.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS layouts
- Purple-heavy gradients
- Decorative glassmorphism
- Overly dense card walls
- All-caps body copy
